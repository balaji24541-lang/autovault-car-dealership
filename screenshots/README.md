Inventory cards in these images are explicitly marked `DEMONSTRATION ONLY`. The temporary screenshot users, vehicles, and purchase row were removed after capture; the existing database inventory was preserved.

- `registration.png` — registration page.
- `inventory.png` — customer inventory filtered to demonstration stock.
- `search-filter.png` — make/model and category filtered result.
- `admin-dashboard.png` — admin management filtered to demonstration stock.
- `add-vehicle.png` — add vehicle form.
- `purchase.png` — successful purchase and updated stock.
